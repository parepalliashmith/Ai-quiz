About AIQUIZ

Learn anything, test yourself — free.

AIQUIZ is a free study companion built for students and competitive-exam aspirants. It turns whatever you are studying — a textbook page, your handwritten notes, a PDF, or just a topic you type — into a set of exam-style practice questions in seconds. Instead of only reading, you actively test what you have learned, which is one of the most effective ways to remember it.

Why we built it

Millions of students in India prepare for exams like UPSC, SSC, banking, railways, state public service commissions, and various entrance tests. Making your own practice questions from a chapter takes a lot of time, and most quiz apps are only in English. We wanted a tool that removes that friction: point your camera at a page, and get a quiz — in English, Telugu, or Hindi. Regional-language learners are often under-served, so first-class Telugu and Hindi support is a core part of AIQUIZ.

What makes it different

  • Scan, don't type: Capture real pages with your camera, add several pages, and get one combined quiz.
  • Any subject: Because it reads your own material, it works for every topic — history, polity, science, mathematics, current affairs and more.
  • Three languages: Take the quiz in English, Telugu (తెలుగు), or Hindi (हिन्दी). A friendly narrator can even read the questions aloud.
  • Track your growth: Scores, averages, best result, and a daily streak keep you motivated. Everything is stored privately on your own device.
  • Exam mode: Turn on negative marking to practise the way real competitive exams score you.

Who it is for

Anyone who studies and wants to test themselves — school and college students, self-learners, and full-time exam aspirants who need fast, unlimited revision practice on their own material.

Our promise on privacy

AIQUIZ does not require an account. Your scores stay on your device, and the pages you scan are only used to create your quiz. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.
