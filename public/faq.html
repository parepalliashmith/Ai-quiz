Frequently asked questions

Is AIQUIZ free to use?

Yes. Scanning pages, typing topics, and generating quizzes are free. The site shows ads to cover its running costs.

Which languages does it support?

The app interface and the quizzes are available in English, Telugu (తెలుగు) and Hindi (हिन्दी). You can keep the app in one language and choose a different language for the quiz itself.

Do I need to create an account?

No. AIQUIZ works without any sign-up. There is nothing to register and no password to remember.

How are the quizzes created?

AIQUIZ uses Google’s Gemini AI to read the page or topic you provide and write exam-style multiple-choice questions, each with a short explanation.

Are my photos or notes stored?

No. The content you scan is used only to generate your quiz and is not saved on our servers afterwards. Your scores are stored privately in your own browser. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I use it for competitive exams?

Yes — that is exactly who it is built for. Choose the difficulty and turn on Exam Mode for negative marking to practise the way exams like UPSC, SSC, banking and railways score you.

Can I scan more than one page?

Yes. Add as many pages as you like and AIQUIZ builds a single quiz covering all of them.

Why is the first load sometimes slow?

The app runs on a free hosting plan that “sleeps” after a period of inactivity. The first visit may take up to a minute to wake up; after that it is fast.

How do I install it on my phone?

Open the app in your browser and choose “Add to Home Screen” (or tap the Install button). It then opens full-screen like a normal app.
