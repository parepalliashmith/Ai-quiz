Logical reasoning — how to crack it

The common question types and a simple method to solve them fast.

Reasoning is the most scoring section for a prepared candidate because it needs no memorisation — only practice and a clear method. Once you recognise the question type, the solution follows a pattern. Here are the types that appear most often.

Series (number and letter)

You are given a sequence and must find the next term or the missing one. Look for a rule: a constant difference, a ratio, squares or cubes, or alternating patterns. For letter series, convert letters to their position numbers (A = 1, B = 2 …) and the hidden pattern usually appears.

Coding–decoding

A word is written in a code and you must decode another word. Compare the letters of the original and the coded word position by position to find the shift (for example, each letter moved forward by three). Apply the same rule to the new word.

Blood relations

These describe a chain of family relationships. Draw a simple family tree with symbols as you read, rather than holding it in your head. Fix who is male or female and the generations, and the answer becomes obvious.

Syllogisms

You are given statements ("All A are B", "Some B are C") and must judge which conclusions follow. Venn diagrams are the safest method: draw the circles exactly as the statements require, and a conclusion is valid only if it is true in every possible diagram.

Direction and seating arrangement

For directions, sketch the north–south–east–west axes and plot each move. For seating, draw the table or row and place people one clue at a time, starting with the most definite clue. A rough diagram prevents most mistakes.

The one habit that works: practice

Reasoning improves only with regular timed practice. Type "Logical Reasoning" or a specific type on the AIQUIZ home page, generate a quiz, and review the explanations to sharpen your method.
