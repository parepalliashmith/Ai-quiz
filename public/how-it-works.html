How AIQUIZ works

From a page to a quiz in under a minute.

1
Scan, upload, or type
Point your camera at a textbook page or your notes and tap the capture button. You can add several pages for one quiz. No camera? Upload an image or PDF, or simply type a topic such as “Indian Polity – Fundamental Rights”.
2
Choose your options
Pick how many questions you want (5–20), the difficulty (easy, medium, hard), and the quiz language — English, Telugu, or Hindi. Turn on Exam Mode if you want negative marking like a real test.
3
Get your quiz
AIQUIZ reads your material and writes exam-style multiple-choice questions. A friendly narrator can read each question and its options aloud in your chosen language.
4
Answer and learn
Pick an answer and instantly see whether it was right, along with a short explanation so you understand the concept — not just the answer.
5
Review and track
At the end you get a score, a full review (with an option to see only the questions you got wrong), and the result is saved to your history so you can watch your average and streak grow over time.

Two quiz types

Study material creates questions from the concepts and facts on your page — perfect for revision. Observe a picture looks at a photo of a room or scene and asks about what is where — a fun way to test observation and attention to detail. If you scan a photo that has no readable text, AIQUIZ automatically switches to observation questions.

Works on any phone

AIQUIZ runs in your browser — nothing to download. You can also install it to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience. Give it a try from the home page.
