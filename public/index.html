Study smarter with AIQUIZ

AIQUIZ turns whatever you are studying into instant practice questions. Instead of only reading a chapter, you test yourself on it — a proven method called active recall that helps you remember far more at exam time. Scan a page, upload a PDF, or type any topic, and get a quiz in seconds.

Built for competitive exam aspirants

Whether you are preparing for UPSC, SSC, banking, railways, state public service commissions, or college entrance tests, AIQUIZ lets you revise unlimited questions from your own study material. Switch on Exam Mode for negative marking so your practice feels like the real test.

Learn in your language

Take quizzes in English, Telugu, or Hindi, and let the built-in narrator read each question aloud. Regional-language learners finally get a study tool made for them.

Track your progress

Every quiz is scored and saved to your private history on this device, so you can watch your average and daily streak grow. No account, no sign-up, no personal details required.
