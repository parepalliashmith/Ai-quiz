Study Coach — tips to score higher

1. Test yourself, don’t just re-read.

Recalling an answer from memory (active recall) locks it in far better than re-reading. Turn every chapter into a quick quiz.

2. Space out your revision.

Revisit a topic after a day, then a few days, then a week. Spaced repetition beats cramming.

3. Practise the exam format.

Use Exam Mode with negative marking so your practice mirrors the real paper.

4. Review your mistakes.

Your wrong answers are your best guide — reread the explanations and revisit weak topics next day.

5. Study in your strongest language.

Take quizzes in English, Telugu, or Hindi — whichever you think in fastest.
