Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

AIQUIZ (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a study tool that turns photos, notes, or topics into practice quizzes. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Information we handle

  • Photos & text you provide: When you scan a page, upload a file, or type a topic, that content is sent to Google’s Gemini AI service to generate quiz questions. We do not store your photos or files on our servers after the quiz is generated.
  • Quiz history & settings: Your scores, streaks, chosen language, and preferences are saved only in your own device’s browser storage (localStorage). They are never uploaded to us and you can delete them any time from within the app.
  • No account required: We do not ask for your name, email, or password, and we do not maintain user accounts.

2. Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks may also be used; you can review their policies where applicable.

3. How the AI processes your content

Quiz generation is powered by Google’s Gemini API. Content you submit is transmitted to Google to produce your quiz and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. We only send what is needed to create the quiz.

4. Data security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because your quiz history stays on your own device, you remain in control of it and can clear it at any time.

5. Children’s privacy

AIQUIZ is intended for students and general users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require any personal details to function.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
