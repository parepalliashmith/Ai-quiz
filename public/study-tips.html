Study tips for competitive exams

Simple, research-backed ways to remember more and score higher.

Preparing for exams like UPSC, SSC, banking, railways, or state public service commissions is as much about how you study as how many hours you put in. Here are the habits that make the biggest difference — and how AIQUIZ helps you build them.

1. Test yourself, don’t just re-read

Reading a chapter again feels productive, but your brain remembers far more when you try to recall the answer from memory. This is called active recall, and it is one of the most reliable study techniques in learning research. Turning a page into a quick quiz forces recall instead of passive reading — which is exactly what AIQUIZ does in seconds.

2. Space out your revision

Instead of cramming a topic once, revisit it after a day, then after a few days, then after a week. This “spaced repetition” fights forgetting. Keep a simple schedule: quiz a topic today, and quiz it again later in the week to lock it in.

3. Practise the exam’s format

Competitive exams are mostly multiple-choice with negative marking. Practising in that exact format builds speed and teaches you when to skip a risky guess. Use Exam Mode in AIQUIZ to add negative marking so your practice mirrors the real paper.

4. Review your mistakes

Your wrong answers are your best study guide. After every quiz, read the explanations for the questions you missed, and note the concepts that keep tripping you up. Revisit only those weak areas the next day — that’s where your marks are hiding.

5. Study in your strongest language

You understand and remember concepts fastest in the language you think in. If that is Telugu or Hindi, take your quizzes in that language. AIQUIZ supports English, Telugu, and Hindi, and can even read questions aloud.

6. Keep short, focused sessions

Short, regular practice beats occasional marathon sessions. Even 10 minutes of self-testing a day adds up. Track your daily streak to stay consistent — small wins build momentum.
